Managed Services

Continuous operations for infrastructure that cannot afford surprises.

Stackly managed services give your organization a dependable operating layer across cloud platforms, applications, networks, data stores, and security controls. We take ownership of monitoring, maintenance, alert triage, patch coordination, incident response, reporting, and ongoing optimization while your internal team stays focused on business priorities. Every engagement starts with a readiness review, a baseline architecture map, and a shared runbook so responsibilities are clear before live support begins.

Our model blends proactive engineering with practical service management. You get named engineers, transparent reporting, and improvement recommendations tied to availability, cost, performance, and risk.

  • Infrastructure monitoring and alert response
  • Cloud resource optimization
  • Patch and vulnerability coordination
  • Backup verification and recovery testing
  • Identity and access review
  • Release support and rollback readiness
  • Monthly executive reporting
  • Incident postmortems and prevention plans
  • Capacity planning
  • Compliance evidence support
Key benefit: Predictable IT operations with fewer escalations, faster recovery, and clearer ownership.

Cloud Migration

Move workloads to cloud with a migration plan built around risk control.

Stackly plans and executes cloud migrations for organizations that need speed without guesswork. We assess current environments, identify dependency risks, define landing zones, migrate workloads in controlled waves, and harden every release with observability, security, and rollback paths. The result is a migration program that protects business continuity while unlocking cloud scalability, improved release cadence, and more predictable operating costs.

Our teams have moved commerce platforms, finance systems, healthcare workloads, analytics pipelines, and internal enterprise applications across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. Each migration includes measurable targets for cost, performance, availability, and user impact.

  1. 01Assess estate, dependencies, risks, and business priorities.
  2. 02Design landing zone, network model, security controls, and data strategy.
  3. 03Pilot representative workloads with monitoring and rollback validation.
  4. 04Migrate in waves with runbooks, testing, and stakeholder checkpoints.
